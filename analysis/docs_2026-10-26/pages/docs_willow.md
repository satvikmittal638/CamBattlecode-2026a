# Willow

A ranged champion who can heal an ally or reset a basic attack, with modest stat growth.

*   Ranged
*   Attack reset
*   Healing & shields

Health

**700**+50 per shard

Strength

**55**+3 per shard

Focus

**140**+5 per shard

Ranged range

**3 tiles**Basic attack

KitOne cast per turn · 8-round cooldowns

**Passive**None. Defined by its stats and two abilities.

    *   Self
